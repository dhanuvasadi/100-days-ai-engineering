# Day 9 - Choosing the Right AI Model

## 🎯 Learning Objective

Understand why different AI models are used for different tasks.

---

# What I Learned

- AI models have different strengths.
- Engineers choose models based on the task.
- Speed, cost, and accuracy all matter.
- Enterprise applications may use multiple models.
- Model selection is an architectural decision.

---

# Enterprise Example

A Data Engineering AI Assistant may use a fast model for documentation summaries and a more capable reasoning model for analyzing multiple ETL pipelines.

---
Companies do not use the largest model for every request because larger models are more expensive, consume more
tokens, and usually have higher response latency. For simple tasks, a smaller and faster model provides sufficient
accuracy at a much lower cost. Companies balance cost, speed, and quality when selecting a model.

---

# Today's Reflection

Today I learned that choosing the right AI model is part of designing an AI system. The best choice depends on the task, not just on using the largest model available.
